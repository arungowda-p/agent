# Pricing

The Starter plan costs $19 per month and includes 3 users. The Pro plan costs $49 per month and includes 10 users.
All plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start the trial.

# Billing

Invoices are emailed on the first day of each billing cycle. You can switch between monthly and annual billing at any time.
Annual billing saves 20 percent. Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase.

# Account

You can reset your password from the login page using the Forgot password link.
To add teammates, open Settings, choose Members and click Invite.

# Support hours

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Eastern Time. Pro customers get priority email support.
